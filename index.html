Yes, I can scope a website chatbot that answers from approved business information, captures enquiries and hands complex questions to a human. The AI provider, hosting, usage limits and ongoing costs are agreed before implementation.

Yes. I am open to remote projects and collaboration. We can agree on communication, scope, timeline, payment method and time-zone expectations before starting.

Yes. The first step is to review a sample with sensitive information removed, understand the workflow and agree on a safe, practical improvement.
